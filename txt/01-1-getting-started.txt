1. Getting started

Every game begins by declaring its canvas.
      Coordinates start at the upper-left corner.
      X increases rightward and Y increases downward.
      The generated webpage scales the canvas to fit
      the available screen while preserving its proportions.

CODE EXAMPLE:
canvas(400,440);
rect(0,0,400,440,"#25221f");
text("Hello QG",20,30,"#ead4b6");

The first two canvas arguments are the logical width and height.
      Drawing uses these coordinates regardless of the phone's
      physical screen resolution.
