3. Math and operators

QG supports arithmetic, comparisons, and logical operations.
      Expressions are encoded as compact binary instructions
      rather than storing their original JavaScript-like text.

CODE EXAMPLE:
let x=10+5*2;
let y=floor(x/3);
let distance=abs(x-y);

if(x>10&&y<20){
  x=x+1;
}

Arithmetic: +, -, *, /, %.
      Comparisons: ==, !=, <, >, <=, >=.
      Logic: &&, ||, !.
      Frequently used small integers have shorter encodings.
