7. Drawing and colors

Use rect to draw filled rectangles, text to display
      labels, clear to clear the canvas, and blit for sprites.
      Rectangles and text can use hexadecimal color strings
      or built-in numeric color IDs.

CODE EXAMPLE:
rect(0,0,400,400,"#25221f");
rect(10,10,50,50,"#665448");
text("Score: "+score,10,420,"#ead4b6");

Hexadecimal colors provide precise control.
      Numeric drawing colors use the runtime's fixed palette;
      sprite palette declarations do not automatically
      change those numeric drawing colors.
      Clearing the canvas makes it transparent, so draw
      a background afterward when necessary.
