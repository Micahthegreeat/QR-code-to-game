10. Binary compression

QG compiles source code into an abstract syntax tree,
      assigns numeric IDs to symbols, encodes operations
      using prefix-free variable-length instructions,
      and packs the resulting bits into Base39.

Common small numbers use short encodings.
      Other integers use variable-length representations.
      Colors use RGB555. Sprite pixels use base-5 packing
      or run-length encoding. Arrays and expressions
      are encoded structurally.

Compression is not guaranteed to make every game fit
      inside one QR code. Large sprites, long strings,
      complex AI and repeated logic still consume space.
      Always inspect the actual compiled Base39 length.
